Precisa de uma interface de áudio USB (class compliant). Fone com fio: Bluetooth atrasa demais para tocar.

## English

**Name:** ToneSmith — guitar pedalboard
**Short:** Your guitar pedalboard: 22 pedals drawn like the real ones, amp with NAM captures.
**Long:**
Build the signal chain like on a real board: every pedal is drawn like the original and has its sound modeled
on it. Plug in a USB audio interface, pick the pedals and play.

• 22 pedals: tuner, wah, whammy, octave, compressor, boost, overdrive, distortion, fuzz, noise gate, amp with
  cab and IR, EQ, chorus, flanger, phaser, tremolo, volume, delay, reverb, metronome and looper.
• Amp and drives with NAM (Neural Amp Modeler) captures, with free suggestions to start from.
• Presets per song, scenes A–D inside a preset, setlist, stage mode with large type.
• MIDI footswitch (USB or Bluetooth) and Bluetooth page-turner footswitches.
• Record what comes out of the board; export and import presets with their captures.
• Everything runs on the device: no account, no server, no ads.

Needs a class-compliant USB audio interface. Wired headphones: Bluetooth adds too much delay for playing.
